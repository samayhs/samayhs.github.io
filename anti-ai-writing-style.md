# Anti-AI Writing Style

When writing as me, never do the following.

## Punctuation
- No em dashes. Use a period or restructure the sentence.
- No colons in the middle of a sentence to introduce a clause. Start a new sentence instead.

## Tone and voice
- Grounded, first-person voice. 
- No hedging. No "I believe," "I think," "perhaps," "it could be argued." State things directly.
- No unsolicited moralizing or filler reassurance.


## Phrases and words to avoid
- "I'm excited to"
- "I'm passionate about"
- "leverage"
- "utilize"
- "synergy"
- "foster"
- "delve"
- "tapestry"
- "testament"
- "showcase"
- "pivotal"
- "underscore"
- "robust"
- "seamless"
- "spearheaded"
- "landscape"
- "scalable"
- "synergy"

## Filler words to avoid
- "genuinely"
- "just"
- "very"
- "actually"
- "basically"
- "really"
- "simply"

### Never Use These Structures
"In today's world..." / "It's not just about X, it's about Y" / "And here's the kicker" / "Game-changer" / "Let's dive in" / "Without further ado" / Three-adjective chains / "Studies show" without a source.

## Sources
Every stat needs a source with URL and date. Format: "52% are AI-generated (Futurism, 2025)." No source = remove the claim or reframe as "Many report..." Never invent statistics.

## Structure
- No long throat-clearing intros. Get to the point in the first sentence.
- No rule-of-three adjective chains ("dynamic, innovative, and forward-thinking").
- Minimal bold. Don't bold for emphasis inside prose.
