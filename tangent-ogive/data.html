// 04 — Grid Convergence

Euler mesh refinement

Three meshes with roughly 4× refinement between levels. The fine-grid result converges to $C_{D,p} = 0.313$ with a Richardson-extrapolated ($h\to0$) value of 0.31175 and an observed order of accuracy $p = 2.8$.

All models compared

Key result: Newtonian theory and Euler CFD predict identical forebody pressure drag ($C_{D,p} = 0.313$). RANS predicts ~9% higher ($C_{D,p} = 0.342$) due to viscous shock–boundary-layer interaction, despite negligible skin friction ($C_{D,f} \approx 2\times10^{-6}$).
