Stiffened Fuselage Section — Skin, Hat Stringers & Pressurization FEA

Parametric NX model → NASTRAN linear static → 3-mesh convergence

A fully parametric fuselage skin section built in NX — diameter, cylinder length, skin thickness, and hat-stiffener count and thickness are all Expression-driven and merged into a single solid body. The section includes a window and a door cutout with filleted corners, modeled after a narrow-body reference geometry.

Loading: uniform internal pressure of 0.07 MPa on the inner skin surface to simulate cabin pressurization, with one end fixed and the other constrained in translation to represent a segment supported by adjacent fuselage bays.

Result: stress concentrated at the window and door corners as expected, with the peak occurring in the skin bridge between the two cutouts — converging to 272.6 MPa max Von-Mises stress and 24.95 mm max displacement, with V2→V3 mesh refinement agreeing to within 5%.

  • Mesh type3D Tetra10
  • Finest mesh628,603 elements / 1.26M nodes
  • Material (skin)Al 2024-T3, E = 73 GPa, ν = 0.33
  • ConvergenceAchieved, V2/V3 < 5% Δ

Bolted Flange Assembly — Mating Parts & Fastener Pattern

Multi-body assembly, McMaster-referenced geometry

A forged butt-weld flange pair modeled after a standard McMaster-Carr pipe-size-2 reference, with an 8-hole bolt circle, a compressed gasket extrusion between mating faces, and a single bolt patterned into all 8 holes at a perfect fit.

Covers assembly-level modeling: mating constraints, pattern features, and fit between parts, rather than a single component in isolation.
